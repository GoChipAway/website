Privacy policy

Effective November 12, 2026

The short version: everything you put in ChipAway stays on your phone. If you turn on sync, a copy is also kept in your own Google Drive, in a folder only ChipAway can open, so you can use your list on a computer too. We don't have servers that receive your data, we don't use analytics or ads, and we don't collect, share or sell anything about you.

What ChipAway stores, and where

ChipAway stores the projects you add (names, costs, times, priorities, difficulty, locations, notes, dates and links between projects), your settings, and any photos you attach. All of it is saved in the app's private storage on your device. If you turn on sync (below), a copy is also kept in your Google Drive. None of it is sent to us.

Sync with Google Drive (optional)

Sync is off until you turn it on in Settings by signing in with Google. When it's on:

  • What's stored: your list, your settings and your photos, the same things listed above.
  • Where: in your own Google Drive, in a hidden app folder that only ChipAway can read. It doesn't appear among your Drive files, and ChipAway can't see anything else in your Drive.
  • Who can see it: you, on the devices where you've signed in to ChipAway with the same Google account, including the web version at gochipaway.com/app. We never receive it: the app and the web page talk to Google directly, and ChipAway has no server in between.
  • What we ask Google for: access to that app folder (the drive.appdata permission) and your email address, which is only shown in Settings so you know which account is syncing.
  • How long: until you delete it. Settings → Sync → Delete my cloud copy removes it from your Drive. You can also remove it in Google Drive under Settings → Manage apps → ChipAway → Delete hidden app data, and take away ChipAway's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google stores the folder under your Google account and its own privacy policy. ChipAway's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we collect

Nothing. ChipAway doesn't require an account, doesn't use analytics or crash-reporting services, doesn't show ads, and doesn't include third-party tracking code. The app works with no internet connection; sync only needs one when it runs.

Photos and the camera

When you choose to add a photo, ChipAway opens your phone's camera or photo picker. The photo you take or pick is resized and saved in the app's private storage. ChipAway doesn't look at the rest of your photo library, and photos never leave your device unless you back them up, share them yourself or turn on sync.

Backups and exports

Back up and Export are things you start yourself. They create a file and open your phone's share sheet so you can choose where it goes, such as Google Drive or email. Once you send a file somewhere, that service's own privacy policy applies to it. Apart from sync, which you turn on yourself, ChipAway doesn't upload anything on its own.

Separately, if you've turned on Android's own device backup, Android may include ChipAway's data in the backup it keeps in your Google account, so your list comes back when you set up a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under your account; we never see it. You can turn it off in your phone's backup settings.

Deleting your data

You can delete any project or photo in the app; with sync on, the delete reaches your Drive copy and your other devices too. Uninstalling ChipAway deletes everything it stored on your phone, but not your Drive copy: delete that in Settings first, or in Google Drive as described above. Because we never receive your data, there's nothing on our side to delete.

Children

ChipAway is meant for adults managing their homes and isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may later add optional household sharing, which would sync a household's list and photos between its members' devices through our own servers. Before that or any other change to how data is handled is released, we'll update this page to explain exactly what's stored, where, and for how long, and change the date at the top.
