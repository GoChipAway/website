Privacy policy

Effective November 18, 2026

The short version: if you use ChipAway without signing in, everything stays on your phone (or in your browser, for the web app) and nothing is sent to us. If you sign in to share your list, your list, photos, name and email address are kept on ChipAway's server so the people you share with can see them. We use them only to run the shared list. We don't use analytics or ads, and we don't sell or share anything about you.

What ChipAway stores, and where

ChipAway stores the projects you add (names, costs, times, priorities, difficulty, locations, notes, dates and links between projects), your settings, and any photos you attach. It's saved in the app's private storage on your device, or in your browser's storage when you use the web app at gochipaway.com/app. Signed out, none of it is sent to us.

Signing in and shared lists (optional)

Signing in is optional, and the app works fully without it. You sign in in Settings, with Google or with a code sent to your email. Once you're signed in:

  • What's stored on our server: your list (everything listed above), the photos you attach, who's working on what, the names of any lists you make and who's on them, and, for each person in a shared list, the name they chose and their email address. If you invite someone, their email address is stored with the invite so they can join.
  • Where: on ChipAway's server, which runs on Supabase, a hosting service that stores the data and handles sign-in for us. Sign-in codes are emailed through Resend, an email delivery service.
  • Who can see it: you, on every phone and computer where you sign in, and the people in your shared list (up to six people in total). Anything you put in Just me is only visible to you, and a list you make for a few people is only visible to them. Security rules on the server enforce this.
  • What we ask Google for: if you sign in with Google, your name and email address, to identify you and to show your name to the people in your list. ChipAway doesn't ask for access to your Google Drive or anything else in your Google account. On the web, the sign-in button is loaded from Google.
  • What we do with it: keep your shared list working and in sync. We don't use it for advertising or profiling, and we don't sell it or give it to anyone else.

Supabase and Resend process this data on our behalf under their own privacy policies (Supabase, Resend). Google sign-in is covered by Google's privacy policy.

What we collect

Signed out, nothing. Signed in, only what's described above, so your shared list can work. ChipAway doesn't use analytics or crash-reporting services, doesn't show ads, and doesn't include third-party tracking code. The app works with no internet connection; syncing a shared list only needs one when it runs.

Photos and the camera

When you choose to add a photo, ChipAway opens your phone's camera or photo picker. The photo you take or pick is resized and saved in the app's private storage. ChipAway doesn't look at the rest of your photo library. Photos never leave your device unless you back them up, share them yourself, or are signed in, in which case they're uploaded to ChipAway's server so the people in your list can see them.

Backups and exports

Back up and Export are things you start yourself. They create a file and open your phone's share sheet so you can choose where it goes, such as Google Drive or email. Once you send a file somewhere, that service's own privacy policy applies to it. Apart from syncing while you're signed in, ChipAway doesn't upload anything on its own.

Separately, if you've turned on Android's own device backup, Android may include ChipAway's data in the backup it keeps in your Google account, so your list comes back when you set up a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under your account; we never see it. You can turn it off in your phone's backup settings.

Deleting your data

You can delete any project or photo in the app. While you're signed in, the delete reaches the server and everyone in your list. The photo itself is removed from the server, and the project is kept only as a short record marked deleted, so the delete reaches every phone.

Sign out (Settings) stops syncing and keeps your list on that device. Leave this list takes you out of a shared list; what you added stays with the list for the people still in it. When the last person leaves a list, it's kept for 30 days in case that was a mistake, and then deleted.

Delete my account (Settings, while signed in) deletes your account right away: your sign-in, your name and email, your Just me items and any list only you are on, with their photos. If you're the only person in your shared list, the whole list goes from the server. If others share it, what you added stays with them, without your name. Your list stays on your device either way.

Can't get into the app? Email gochipaway@gmail.com from the address you sign in with, and we'll delete your account the same way within 30 days. Uninstalling ChipAway deletes everything it stored on your phone, but not what's on the server.

Children

ChipAway is meant for adults managing their homes and isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

Before any change to how data is handled is released, we'll update this page to explain exactly what's stored, where, and for how long, and change the date at the top.
