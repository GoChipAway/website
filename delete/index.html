Delete your ChipAway account

You only have an account if you signed in to share your list. Without signing in, everything is on your device, and uninstalling the app (or clearing your browser's data, for the web app) removes it.

In the app or on the web

  1. Open ChipAway on your phone, or go to gochipaway.com/app on any computer or phone and sign in the way you usually do (Google or an email code).
  2. Open Settings (the gear at the top).
  3. Under your shared list, tap Delete my account, read what it removes, and tap Delete my account again.

It happens right away. You don't need to reinstall the app to do it.

Can't sign in?

Email gochipaway@gmail.com from the address you sign in with and ask us to delete your account. We'll delete it the same way within 30 days and reply when it's done.

What's deleted

  • Your sign-in, and your name and email address as the people in your list see them
  • Your Just me items, and any list only you are on, with their photos
  • Invites sent to your email address, and your marks on who's working on what
  • If you're the only person in your shared list: the whole list on our server, with its photos

What stays

  • Things you added to a list that other people share stay with them, without your name.
  • The copy of your list on your own devices. Delete the app (or your browser's data) to remove it.
  • Any backup or export files you saved yourself.

More detail is in the privacy policy.
